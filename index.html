Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about accessing and using b1t-Sched.

How does b1t-Sched work?

b1t-Sched groups students by their academic identity — Department, Semester, and Section. All users who share the same academic combination automatically see the section-level Pending Tasks and Class Notices.

Student vs. Faculty Login & Sign-Up

Students: Sign up with your university email. In Set Details, provide your 10–16 digit Student ID along with your Department, Semester, and Section. You can log in using either your Student ID or your registered email address.

Faculty: Toggle the Faculty button during login or sign up. Faculty accounts operate department-wide without semester/section constraints and can log in using their official Name Initial (e.g. ABC) or registered email.

What are the platform roles?
  • Admin — Full platform authority across tasks, notices, and user role management.
  • DptCoor (Department Coordinator) — Faculty leadership role with department-wide coordination and notice posting privileges.
  • Faculty — Department-wide task and notice tracking without semester/section constraints. Verified through the approval management system.
  • CR (Class Representative) — Class leadership role capable of posting section-level tasks, exams, and class notices.
  • Student — Standard academic user accessing section-specific pending tasks and notices.
Email verification & password resets

After creating an account, verify your email address via the confirmation link sent to your inbox. If you forget your password, click the Forgot Password? link on the login screen to receive a secure password reset email.

    FAQ
    How does b1t-Sched work?

    b1t-Sched groups students by their academic identity — Department, Semester, and Section. All users who share the same combination see the same section-level "Pending Tasks" and section-level Class Notices.

    For Faculty and Department Coordinators, tasks and notices operate on a department-wide level, allowing them to manage academic tasks and view department notices without semester or section constraints.

    However, task checkboxes are individual — marking a task as complete only affects your own dashboard. Other users in the same group won't see your completion status.

    Events are shown based on department (or marked as "ALL" for university-wide events) and are visible in the sidebar on desktop or in the slide-out panel on mobile.

    What are the user roles?

    There are five specialized roles in addition to regular students:

    • Admin — Full platform authority: can create, edit, or delete any task, notice, or event across all departments, manage user accounts, assign or remove roles, and access the administrative dashboard.
    • DptCoor (Department Coordinator) — Faculty leadership role with department-level coordination capabilities. Can post, edit, and manage Department Notices for their entire department, create department-wide events, and manage faculty tasks. Operates department-wide without semester/section constraints.
    • Faculty — Can view and complete department-wide tasks and read Department Notices. Can create and manage department events and add faculty tasks/meetings. Cannot post Department Notices (requires the DptCoor role) and operates without semester or section profile restrictions.
    • CR (Class Representative) — Student leadership role for a specific section group. Can post, edit, and manage Class Notices (CR Notices) for their section, delete or reset any task in their section, and create department events.
    • Regular Student / User — Can add section tasks, edit/delete their own submitted tasks and events, read Class & University Notices, and mark tasks as complete on their personal dashboard.
    • Blocked — Restricted to read-only access. Cannot add, edit, or delete tasks, notices, or events, cannot modify profile settings, and sees a restriction banner on the dashboard.
    Profile settings & changes

    You can change your academic details (department, semester, section) from the Profile Settings page, accessible by clicking your user card in the navigation bar.

    Important: Profile changes are limited to once every 30 days to prevent misuse. If you need an earlier change, please contact the Admin.

    Your Student ID is set during initial registration and cannot be changed through the profile settings.
