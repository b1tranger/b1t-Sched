# Implementation Plan - Classroom Window Border Padding & 3-Group Assignment Layout

Fix the unwanted border padding on smaller desktop displays for the Google Classroom window, and restructure the To-Do assignments section to categorize tasks into three distinct groups (Assigned / No Due Date, Upcoming / Yet to pass due date, and Passed due date with a small-height divider banner), plus a clean section for Completed tasks.

## User Review Required

> [!NOTE]
> - **Group 1**: Assigned tasks (no due date set) displayed at the top.
> - **Group 2**: Tasks that have yet to pass their due date (upcoming deadlines), sorted with earliest due dates first.
> - **Group 3**: Tasks that have passed their due date (missing/overdue), divided by a compact, high-contrast banner header.
> - **Completed Group**: Tasks that are turned in, graded, or returned will display neatly below with their own completed divider.
